# Quality, accessibility and moderation
Audit source-traceability, completeness, factual correctness, spellings, equations, mark allocations, choice parity, question numbering, duplication within and across sets, coverage and outcome/difficulty balance. Check age, language and reading load, plausible completion time, answer-space considerations and use of accessible descriptions for diagrams. Preserve important educational distinctions; avoid misleading oversimplification. Provide differentiated access and inclusive language when appropriate. Secure unreleased examiner keys if the user requests separation. Avoid importing identifiable student scores or names into a generic template. External material must be referenced accurately; original question writing is preferred over copying protected exam banks. Indicate unresolved items explicitly; 'passed structural audit' does not guarantee academic approval.
