# Flexible syllabus extraction
Read full scope, not just top headings. Preserve supplied unit/module hierarchy and exact topic labels, optional/mandatory exclusions, prerequisites, learning outcomes, competency statements, references and allocation of hours, credits or marks when present. Map source page/section per topic. For scanned syllabus or figures, inspect image pages and check symbols/table borders, rather than relying on a broken text extraction. Normalize spelling only for an internal alias; keep canonical source terms in documents when required. Mark `unknown` when a rule is absent. In interdisciplinary syllabi, distinguish teaching scope from assessed scope. Avoid inventing a canonical course-code system or compulsory CO/PO/Bloom mapping if not in supplied requirements. Maintain an exception log for overlaps, missing topic descriptions and contradictions.
